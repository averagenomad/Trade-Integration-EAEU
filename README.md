## **Overview**  
This repository contains the final paper and outputs for the project:  

**The Effect of Eurasian Economic Union on the Trade Volume of its Member States in the Dynamics of Social Network**  
*Claremont Graduate University*  

The study examines the impact of the **Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU)** on the trade volumes of its member states, analyzing trade dynamics two years prior to the formation of the union and seven years after. The research uses **social network analysis (SNA) metrics** to evaluate changes in trade relationships and incorporates these metrics into regression models to test for statistical significance.  

### **Key Points**  
- Uses bilateral trade data between EAEU members and the rest of the world.  
- Generates **social network metrics** using **Gephi**, integrating them into regression analysis.  
- Provides descriptive visualizations of the trade network over time and evaluates how trade volumes and connectivity changed post-union formation.
